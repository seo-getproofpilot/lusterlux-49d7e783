Waterless wash & surface protectant · 16 oz

LuxPro

LuxPro cleans light dust, pollen, fingerprints and road film without a hose, bucket or pressure washer. Its high-lubricity formula surrounds the dirt so it lifts into the towel instead of scratching across the paint. NanoFusion polymers then leave a slick, glossy layer that helps dust slide off between washes.

How to use
Spray one panel, wipe with an Edgeless microfiber towel, then buff with a dry side.
Coverage
15 to 30 vehicles per bottle
Safe on
Paint, glass, chrome, plastic trim, wheels, PPF and ceramic coatings
Best for
Light dust and fingerprints between washes. Rinse off caked mud first.
$19.99

Ceramic detail spray · 16 oz

CeramicX

CeramicX bonds a hydrophobic ceramic layer to your paint. Water beads and sheets off, dirt and road grime release more easily on the next wash, and the finish picks up depth and gloss. One application protects for up to 8 months, and it tops up an existing ceramic coating.

How to use
On a clean, dry surface, spray, spread evenly with an Edgeless microfiber towel, then buff.
Coverage
10 to 20 vehicles per bottle
Lasts
Up to 8 months per application
Safe on
Paint, clear coat, chrome, plastic trim, PPF and existing ceramic coatings
$27.99
