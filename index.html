Waterless wash + surface protectant

LuxPro

No hose. No bucket. No pressure washer.

Spray a panel, wipe it with an Edgeless towel, flip to a dry side and buff. LuxPro wraps light dust, pollen and fingerprints so they lift off instead of dragging, then leaves a slick layer that keeps dust from settling back in.

New to detailing? Start here. If you can wipe a counter, you can use LuxPro.

  • No water needed
  • 15–30 cars per bottle
  • Leaves a dust-repelling layer
  • Safe on paint, PPF & ceramic
$19.99

Ceramic detail spray

CeramicX

Water beads. Gloss deepens. Up to 8 months.

After a wash, mist a panel, spread it with an Edgeless towel and buff it clear before you move on. CeramicX bonds a hydrophobic layer to paint, trim and chrome, so water runs off, dirt lets go easier and the next wash goes faster. Already ceramic coated? It tops the coating up.

Best once washing is routine. Work in the shade, one panel at a time, and buff before it dries.

  • Up to 8 months per coat
  • 10–20 cars per bottle
  • Water beads and sheets off
  • Safe on PPF & existing coatings
$27.99
